# Clients, events, and bookings

For Glow Booth / HALO photobooth gigs, art show events, sponsors, and venue work.

## Every booking needs

- **Date, times, location, and exact setup/teardown window**, plus what the venue must provide
  (power, table, wifi, load-in access). Most day-of disasters are venue assumptions, not equipment.
- **Deposit to hold the date** — non-refundable, and say so. A verbal hold is not a booking.
- **Balance due date** — before the event, not after. Chasing payment from a client whose event is
  already over is the worst collections position there is.
- **Cancellation and reschedule policy**, including what happens if the client cancels late, and
  force majeure (weather, venue closure).
- **Overtime rate** if the event runs long, stated per hour.
- **Usage rights for photos and footage** — whether Malik may use event images in marketing, and
  whether guests' images are covered. Get this right; it also affects what the client may do with
  the images.
- **Client's logo and brand usage** — permission to use their marks on the Tap-to-Start screens and
  overlays, and their approval process for graphics.
- **Damage and liability** — who is responsible for equipment damaged by guests, insurance
  requirements, and a liability cap at fees paid.
- **Point of contact** with authority to approve on-site changes.

## Sponsors and vendors at the art show

- What the sponsor gets, exactly: placement, logo size, mentions, booth, comps — written out.
- Payment before the event, with deliverables tied to payment.
- Approval rights: sponsors approve their own materials, not Malik's programming.
- Exclusivity: does a sponsor block competitors? For how long, in what category?
- Artist agreements: commission split, who sets pricing, who handles sales and sales tax, insurance
  on the work, and what happens to unsold pieces.

## Recurring clients

If a client books repeatedly, use a master services agreement with per-event order forms. Terms are
negotiated once; each booking becomes a one-page form. Faster to book and harder to erode.
